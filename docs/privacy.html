PebbleMeet Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-22 · Maintainer: Alex Palson

PebbleMeet is an open-source Pebble smartwatch watchapp that lets you view upcoming Google Calendar meetings, browse Google Contacts, and start or control Google Meet calls from your wrist.

1. Google User Data We Access

When you choose to connect your Google account in PebbleMeet Settings, we request only the minimum read/create scopes required for watchapp functionality:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.events.readonly — reads upcoming events with Google Meet links from your primary calendar to display on your Pebble watch.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/contacts.readonly — reads contact names and email addresses so you can initiate a Google Meet call from your watch menu.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/meetings.space.created — creates a new Google Meet space URL when you start an ad-hoc call to a contact.
  • email — displays which Google account is currently signed in on the settings screen.

2. How Data Is Stored & Processed

  • On-device storage only: Your OAuth access token and refresh token are stored locally inside the Pebble mobile app's PebbleKit JS storage on your phone.
  • Stateless OAuth relay: Our Cloudflare Worker relay exchanges authorization codes and refreshes expired access tokens on your behalf. It has no database and never stores, logs, or shares your tokens, calendar events, or contacts.
  • No third-party sharing or advertising: PebbleMeet does not sell, share, or transfer Google user data to third parties or use it for advertising or AI/ML training.

3. Revoking Access & Deleting Data

You can disconnect at any time by tapping Disconnect inside PebbleMeet Settings in the Pebble app, or by revoking access directly in your Google Account Permissions. Uninstalling PebbleMeet from the Pebble app removes all locally stored tokens and settings.

4. Open Source & Contact

Full source code for both the watchapp and OAuth relay is available at https://github.com/senorsunday/pebble-meet-watchapp. For privacy questions or deletion requests, please open an issue on that repository.
